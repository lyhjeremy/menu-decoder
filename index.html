Menu Decoder Local LoRA fine-tune / benchmarked against Claude

A 1.5B model that tags menu dishes for 14 allergens from the dish name alone

Photograph a menu in any language and say your allergies and diet. Menu Decoder translates each dish and tags it for cuisine, the 14 EU allergens, spice and vegetarian or vegan. The tagger is Qwen2.5-1.5B with a LoRA adapter that learned the whole JSON schema, so its prompt is one line: Dish: Pad Thai.

On 303 held-out dishes it returns valid tags every time and catches 69.3% of the allergens a dish contains. Claude does better on allergens (79.2%) but only when the full schema is written into every prompt; without it, Claude answers a different question. Because the tagger misses some allergens, the app never marks one as safe.

How it works

Extract
One vision call reads the whole menu into a structured list of dishes, translations and prices. Lines it can't read stay in the list, flagged as unreadable.
Brief
Your spoken or typed constraints become a structured diet brief.
Tag
The local LoRA model tags each dish, prompted to answer may whenever an allergen is plausible.
Filter
filter_safe_dishes drops every dish marked contains or may for your allergens, or failing your diet.
Pick
The LLM recommends two or three dishes from what is left. Tap a dish to hear its name in the menu's language.
